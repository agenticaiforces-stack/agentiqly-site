Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use AgentiQly.io, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or high-contrast settings.

Our goal

We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. We don't claim the site fully meets that standard: no outside audit has been done.

What we checked, and when

Checked 31 October 2026: an automated check (axe-core 4.10, WCAG 2.1 A and AA rules) of the home page, ventures list and venture pages, plus a keyboard-only pass of the menu and links.

The automated check found no problems on the pages tested.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on the site doesn't work for you, write to moshe@agentiqly.io. Say which page and what happened. We'll reply, and if we can't fix it quickly we'll help you get what you needed another way.
