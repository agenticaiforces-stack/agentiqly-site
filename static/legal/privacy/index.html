Privacy policy

This page says what AgentiQly.io collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it corrected or deleted. It describes what the site does today, not what it might do one day.

Who we are

AgentiQly.io (https://www.agentiqly.io) is operated by AgentiQly.io. It is a plain website that describes the ventures AgentiQly.io builds and links to each one. It has no sign-in, no forms and no shop. For anything on this page, write to moshe@agentiqly.io.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhenWhy
Your email address and whatever you writeOnly if you email usTo reply to you

Collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, recorded in the standard logs of our hosting and network providers. They are used to deliver pages and to block attacks.

What we don't collect

  • No analytics, no advertising tags, no tracking pixels.

Who else receives it

A few service providers handle information for us so the site can run. Each receives only what its job needs:

  • Our website host and network provider: the standard request logs described above
  • Our email provider: emails you send us

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. There are no advertising networks or data brokers on this site. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Cookies and browser storage

The cookie notice lists every cookie and storage item this site uses.

"Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control

Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. AgentiQly.io does not track visitors across other websites and runs no advertising, so there is nothing for those signals to switch off. Every visit is treated the same way whether or not a signal is sent.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

There is no account to manage. Or write to moshe@agentiqly.io and say what you want: a copy, a correction, or deletion. We will confirm it's you, then do it within 30 days. This applies wherever you live, California included, and you won't be treated differently for asking.

How long we keep it

  • Emails: as long as needed to deal with what you wrote about, then deleted on request.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting and network providers for their standard periods.

Children

AgentiQly.io is not meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new effective date.
